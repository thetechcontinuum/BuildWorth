# 40-Attribute Opportunity Blueprint Specification

Every opportunity page published on BuildWorth contains 40 standardized attributes:

1. Opportunity title
2. One-sentence summary
3. Problem statement
4. Jobs-to-be-done
5. Proposed product
6. Narrow MVP scope
7. Target customer segments
8. Economic buyer
9. End user
10. Buying trigger
11. Existing workflow or workaround
12. Pain severity
13. Pain frequency
14. Evidence of demand
15. Evidence of willingness to pay
16. Existing competitors
17. Indirect competitors and substitutes
18. Competitor weaknesses
19. Market attractiveness
20. Buyer accessibility
21. Technical feasibility
22. Required technologies and integrations
23. Data-access requirements
24. Legal, regulatory, privacy, and platform risks
25. Estimated MVP build cost range
26. Estimated time to MVP
27. Estimated monthly operating cost
28. Plausible pricing model and price range
29. Customer benefit calculation
30. Reachable market estimate
31. Break-even scenarios
32. Go-to-market channels
33. First 20 customer strategy
34. Defensibility possibilities
35. Major assumptions
36. Major risks
37. Evidence Confidence score
38. Opportunity Score
39. Recommended next validation experiment
40. Linked evidence and sources
